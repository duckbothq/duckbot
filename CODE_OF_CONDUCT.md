# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume good faith. Criticise ideas and code, not the person who brought them.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, particularly with people who are new, or who are not working in their first language. This project is built in Hong Kong and its community spans several languages — imperfect English is not a deficiency.
- Accept that technical disagreement is normal and healthy. Argue the substance.
- Accept a maintainer's decision gracefully when a proposal is declined, even if you disagree.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or behaviour, including in jokes
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind
- Publishing anyone's private information without permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — repository, issues, discussions, pull requests — and when representing the project publicly.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to **info@igears.net**. Reports are handled confidentially, and we will not discuss a report with anyone who does not need to know about it.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

Maintainers who do not follow this code themselves may face the same consequences, decided by the other maintainers.
