# Editions and services

Duckbot Community is the Apache-2.0 open-source core in this repository. Anyone may
use it commercially, adapt it and redistribute it under that licence. Buying support
or a Business subscription is not required to use Community.

| Offering | Scope | Availability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Community | Local sensitive-data detection and masking, local restoration, pre-send preview, basic approvals, local task/audit records, model routing and the scoped read-only file connector. | Developer preview; see the release notes. |
| Business | Planned, separately licensed modules for central device and policy management, organisational roles and approvals, consolidated reporting and managed integrations. | In planning; these capabilities are not included in this repository or currently sold as completed features. |
| iGears Deployment & Care | Scoped evaluation, installation, configuration, integration, training and ongoing support for an agreed environment. | Contact iGears to discuss a paid preview pilot and its acceptance criteria. |

## Community commitments

- Basic privacy protection, local previews and approvals, access to your local records,
  and core security fixes remain part of Community.
- Community does not require an iGears account, licence server, relay or mandatory telemetry.
- Model-provider charges are separate. Bring your own supported API account, or configure
  a real local model; an existing consumer AI subscription does not imply API access.
- Planned Business modules will be developed separately and consume Community interfaces,
  rather than turn this repository into a time-limited trial.
- The Duckbot name and logo are governed separately by the [trademark policy](TRADEMARK.md).

## Services

Deployment projects and optional annual Care are quoted separately. Quotes define the
workflow, devices, deliverables, training, support hours and acceptance criteria. Future
Business software subscriptions will be separate from project, hardware and model costs.
No published Business price or general-availability date is promised here.
